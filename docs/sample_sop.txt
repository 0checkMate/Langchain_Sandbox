SAMPLE SOP-001: COLD-PRESS JUICE PRODUCTION HYGIENE (FICTIONAL EXAMPLE)

1. Purpose
This procedure defines hygiene controls for the cold-press juice line.

2. Personnel hygiene
All staff must wash hands for at least 20 seconds before entering the production area.
Hairnets, beard covers and clean aprons are mandatory. Jewellery is not permitted.

3. Equipment sanitation
Press cloths and contact surfaces are sanitised with a food-grade sanitiser diluted to 200 ppm.
The required contact time is 2 minutes, followed by a potable water rinse.
Sanitation is recorded on form QA-12 at the end of every shift.

4. Product storage
Finished juice is stored at 4 degrees Celsius or below.
The assigned shelf life is 5 days from the production date.
Any batch stored above 6 degrees Celsius for more than 2 hours is quarantined for QA review.

5. Deviations
Any deviation from this procedure is reported to the Production Lead within 1 hour.
